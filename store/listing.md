## App name (max 30)

## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)

Pocket Ledger keeps your bills, expenses and income in one place, on your phone. No account, no
sign-up, no ads.

SCAN A BILL
Take a photo or use the document scanner: the amount, date, shop, tax and bill number are read
for you, on the phone. The photo stays with the entry.

IMPORT BANK STATEMENTS
Import the CSV or PDF statement from your bank. Every booking is listed; untick the ones you
don't want, and bookings you already recorded are recognised.

CATEGORIES THAT LEARN
Categories are guessed from the payee and description: REWE goes to Groceries, Netflix to
Subscriptions. Pick one yourself and tap Remember, and it's filled in next time. Add, rename,
merge or hide categories as you like.

INSIGHTS
See spending by category, income and expenses per month, and how your savings grow. Compare with
the previous period, choose any month or range, and tap a category to see the biggest payees.
Money moved to investments counts as saved, not spent.

PROFILES
Keep personal money apart from a club, a side business or a household. Switch with one tap, or
view everything together.

STATEMENTS
Create a PDF or CSV statement for any period and share it. Mixed currencies can be combined using
what your card was actually charged or daily exchange rates.

BANK NOTIFICATIONS (OPTIONAL)
When a banking app you choose shows a payment, Pocket Ledger can suggest it as an entry. Only the
apps you pick are read, and nothing leaves your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data is stored only on your phone
• Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Backup to a single file, protected with a password if you like, to move to a new phone
• No ads, no tracking
